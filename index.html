About Me

An Nguyen (Anthony) is currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at the School of Computing, Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology, South Korea. He received his B.Eng. in Control Engineering and Automation with honors from Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Vietnam. His research interests include on-device AI, sensor fusion, Internet of Things, and robust localization.

I am curious about how intelligent systems move from ideas into real-world devices. I enjoy exploring the space between algorithms, sensors, and hardware, where small engineering details can shape whether a system works reliably outside the lab.
